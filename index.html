INTRODUCTION

Welcome to ALADDIN AGENCY LIMITED, a recruitment and manpower agency based in Kasarani, Nairobi, Kenya. We are dedicated to connecting job seekers with employment opportunities and helping candidates prepare for the recruitment process.

We believe that every individual deserves access to reliable employment information, professional guidance, and opportunities to build a better future. Our goal is to help candidates understand employer expectations and prepare themselves before meeting prospective employers.

Through professional communication, candidate orientation, and recruitment support, we aim to build positive connections between job seekers and employers locally and internationally.

ABOUT OUR AGENCY

ALADDIN AGENCY LIMITED focuses on recruitment support, candidate orientation, and international employment opportunities. We guide candidates on workplace expectations, interview preparation, required documentation, and professional conduct before they meet their prospective employers.

Our commitment is to professionalism, transparency, responsible recruitment practices, and quality customer service. We encourage candidates to verify job offers and understand the terms of employment before accepting any opportunity.

OUR SERVICES

  • Recruitment and manpower services
  • Candidate orientation before employer interviews
  • Interview preparation and career guidance
  • Information about international job opportunities
  • Employment documentation guidance
  • Connecting suitable candidates with employers

OUR PROJECTS AND GOALS

Our goal is to facilitate recruitment for up to 200 candidates per month for suitable international employment opportunities, subject to employer demand, candidate qualifications, and applicable legal requirements.

Our target destinations include:

  • Qatar
  • Dubai, United Arab Emirates
  • Serbia

We aim to prepare candidates for the recruitment process and connect qualified applicants with legitimate employers.

OUR MISSION

To connect people with meaningful employment opportunities through professional recruitment support, candidate preparation, and responsible international placement services.

OUR VISION

To become a trusted recruitment agency recognized for professionalism, integrity, and helping people access employment opportunities across borders.
